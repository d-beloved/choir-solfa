Choir Solfa

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Choir Solfa turns a song into a four-part tonic solfa score for soprano, alto, tenor and bass. It is a free, open-source project made by Morayodeji. This page says what it keeps about you, why, and for how long. In short: only what it needs to sign you in and make your songs, nothing is sold, and there are no adverts or tracking.

Your account

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and get the codes that confirm your address or reset your password.
  • Your password, if you choose one. It is handled by Amazon Cognito, the sign-in service Choir Solfa uses, and nobody running Choir Solfa can see it.
  • If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells Choir Solfa your name, your email address and whether Google has confirmed that address. Nothing else from your Google account is asked for or seen.

Your songs

  • Links. Choir Solfa downloads the song from the link you paste to make the score. For a Spotify link it reads the song's title, artist and length from Spotify's public page and finds the same recording on YouTube.
  • Audio files you upload are deleted as soon as your song is made, and within 2 days in any case.
  • Scores (the PDF, MIDI and text files, and the practice tracks, which include a lower-quality copy of the recording) are deleted 15 days after they are made, or sooner if you delete the song.
  • The list of your songs (each song's title, the link or file name, when it was made and the settings used) is kept so you can see what you have made before. It doesn't include the music.
  • Your songs are private: other people using Choir Solfa can't see them. The site's admins can see the list of everyone's songs, to keep the service running and fix problems.

In your browser

Choir Solfa keeps you signed in for up to 30 days with one secure cookie, so you don't have to sign in every visit. Signing out removes it. Your browser also remembers that you are signed in and your choice of light or dark colours. Choir Solfa uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Who else is involved

  • Amazon Web Services runs the site and stores accounts, songs and files, in the United States (Ohio).
  • Google, only if you sign in with Google.
  • Cloudflare carries song downloads from links, and the site's address.
  • YouTube and Spotify are where songs from links come from.

The service keeps technical logs (such as errors while making a song) for one month, to fix problems. Your information is not sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Information from Google

Choir Solfa's use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Your name and email address from Google are used only to sign you in to Choir Solfa and to show which account you are using. They are not shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Deleting things

You can delete any song from your list at any time. To delete your account and the list of your songs, or to ask anything about this policy, open an issue on Choir Solfa's GitHub page.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
